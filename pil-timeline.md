# pil-timeline

Structural evidence of accumulation. The practice running before it was tested, tested before it was named, named before it was understood.

This is not a biography. It is a demonstration. Read it as a PIL evaluation of the practice that produced PIL.

---

## 2001 — The Practice Begins

Music production starts. No framework. No name. No institution. The accumulated work of making — sound by sound, track by track — begins building the pattern authority that will take nineteen years to fully name.

This is preponderance at its origin point. Not instruction received. Not a curriculum followed. A practice started and sustained by the practitioner alone.

---

## 2007 — The Field Opens

One week in May. A track finished for a place in Paris. A walk outside at 5am. A garden. A moment of oneness never experienced before.

Then a search. Then Ginsberg. Then everything coming together.

*"This was the moment I had been waiting for."*

This is not the beginning of a project. It is the reorganisation of everything around a new centre of gravity. A life break, not a career break. The distinction is the whole origin of PIL in four words.

The resonance axis activates for the first time as a named experience. Not yet named as resonance. Named as 5am. A moment that can happen at any time. When everything falls into place.

---

## 2008 — The First Release

**Project 5am — Quixotes Of Moons Fight The Windmills Of Brixton**
Monotonik netlabel, mtk205. August 24, 2008.

One year after the peak experience. The practice produces its first public work. Three external readers confirm what is operating without access to any of the practice's own vocabulary.

The Netlabel Archive: genre placement confirmed. Deep house, IDM, chillout. The work belongs to a real field and is heard as belonging to it. Schema fit.

Pedro Leitão, Test Tube: lineage reading. Classic IDM, trip hop, UK garage, 2step synthesised into something coherent. Two favoured tracks — Crepuscular and Wet Ashtray — both threshold titles heard as threshold experiences. The reviewer ends with a seasonal threshold. He felt the quality without knowing why. Resonance.

Katarokkar: *"Project 5am is best taken with a clear mind and a pair of headphones. This is music for audio spacemen."* The perceptual prerequisite stated by a listener with no access to the framework. Specific quality of attention required. Maya Deren identified in the visual lineage without being told. *Audio spacemen* — the conceptual location the practice will later name for itself, found from outside first. Alignment.

All five axes running in the first public release. No names for them yet. The work passes its own evaluation criteria before those criteria have been articulated.

## 2009 — The First Public Articulation

FAD Magazine interview. October 2009.

The practice describes itself in PIL's own terms before PIL exists.

On the resonance axis: *"Seeing, hearing, or reading something great that is linked to the idea of 5am."* Material drawn toward the field rather than searched for.

On preponderance: *"I submerge all the information that I've gained and over time the output will come through."* Pattern authority accumulating beneath conscious reasoning until output emerges from depth.

On the generative application: *"The essence of Project 5am is to explore the feeling of being in the moment, so this can be portrayed in many other ways."* The framework always portable. Never medium-specific. Always about the quality of perception.

On anti-calcification: Miranda July's yellow adopted entirely. The thing that makes everything come alive after you've given up. The instinct to let what resonates in fully rather than holding it at analytical distance.

On the biographical anchor: *"My art is being made so that I have a home I can go to."* Eleven words. The entire foundation.

On the origin: *"Without a doubt having the 5am experience was a life break."*

On the project's nature: *"Every time I feel that I have come to the end of this project some magical story appears out of nowhere so I guess it's an eternal project."*

It was. The field was already generating what it would take years to name.

## 2010 — The Axiom Sent

October 14, 2010. An email to R. Stevie Moore.

The Project 5am axiom appears in writing for the first time — sent directly to another practitioner as an introduction, before anything else is said. Before the website link. Before the archive link. Before the description.

The condition created before the content named. PIL's generative application operating as instinct, six years before Flux Without Pause would make it explicit as a principle.

The email arrives because R. Stevie Moore followed his bliss — Slapp Happy to David Crosby to the Vatican top ten to Donald Fagen to Virginia Astley to *Me You and Everybody We Know* to CD Baby to Deux to Solid Space to Ariel Pink to Project 5am. Eleven steps. No search. Pure resonance field navigation.

*"I found you by not looking for you — that's the beauty of music."*

Miranda July's yellow principle — adopted into the practice from the same film whose soundtrack formed a waypoint in Moore's chain — arrives back as *stay yellow* at the end of his email. The field coherent enough that someone following it blindly ended up inside it, carrying one of its own principles as a greeting.

R. Stevie Moore: godfather of lo-fi, fifty years of practice built entirely from preponderance outside institutional structures. *"nice stuff, like a lot."* From someone who had heard everything. Sufficient.

The resonance field is confirmed as real, bidirectional, and operating without instruction in both directions simultaneously.

## 2012 — The Third Space Applied

*Some Kind of Wonderful* — a short film selected as one of 142 works for Lars Von Trier's Gesamt community project at the Copenhagen Art Festival.

The third space methodology applied directly: encounter with an existing work generates a new one in the interval between. The 間 principle operating four years before Surf/ace would name it. The particle — the discrete moment of contact — producing something neither the original work nor the response could have produced alone.

The practice operating internationally, inside a real curatorial context, producing work from the interval rather than from instruction.

## 2014–2015 — The Confrontation and Its Sustained Duration

An institutional confrontation in the final year of a BA Fine Art degree. Not a neutral period of the practice — the moment of maximum academic vulnerability. Final major project, degree classification, everything accumulated across the entire course on the line simultaneously.

The incident: a prominent artist, invited to speak at the institution, made offensive comments about slavery during a critique of *12 Years A Slave*. The comments were received with laughter by the audience. Jason was the only Black student in the room — and also working at the event. Present in a dual capacity that made exit or non-response structurally more complicated.

The institutional response: freedom of speech. The artist's comments isolated from the crowd's response. The laughter — the collective institutional response in the room — disappeared from the official account. The structural concern repositioned as a matter of individual speech. The person raising it repositioned as the problem.

The confrontation did not end when the course ended. The final results were affected by participants in the incident. An appeal ran for fifteen months after graduation — into what should have been the period of transition into practice. The institutional machinery sustained against the work and the person simultaneously, across the entire final year and fifteen months beyond it.

The confrontation is not what produced PIL. This is the revision the archive forces. By 2014 the practice had been accumulating for thirteen years. The field had opened in 2007. The first release had passed all five axes in 2008. The axiom had been sent as a generative instrument in 2010. The third space had been applied internationally in 2012. The practice was already structurally sound. The confrontation proved it by failing to break it.

What 2014 produced was Flux Without Pause — the wave, the anti-calcification mechanism, the framework that emerged from unplanned conflict applying existing Project 5am frameworks against institutional barriers. The album *--k̶a̶i̶r̶o̶s̶--In--Chronos--* released November 2014. Fifteen tracks. The title is the PIL reading of the period — Kairos, the qualitative moment of clarity, held inside Chronos, the sustained duration of institutional time grinding forward. Track 4: Intersectionality.

While the appeal was still active, two academic papers were presented at major UK institutions documenting the case in real time.

**SOAS, University of London — November 2014**
*Intersectionality: A Space for Theoretical and Practitioner Discussion*
The surface diversity critique stated publicly: the institution uses Black and minority ethnic students' cultural background via statistics and images to promote a surface level and false notion of diversity. The gap between stated identity and preponderance named as a structural argument at one of the UK's most significant academic institutions, while the appeal was still running.

**CERES Conference, University of Edinburgh — June 2015**
*Invisible Visible: A case study of one university's handling of racism/discrimination*
The full structural argument presented to an academic conference. The crowd's laughter named alongside the artist's comments as the institutional event — not an individual's speech act but a collective response that revealed the institution's actual culture. The freedom of speech defence identified as the deflection move.

The external inquiry, commissioned as part of the aftermath, confirmed in writing everything argued at SOAS and Edinburgh. Nineteen recommendations addressed to the institution. None to the student. The structural concern was correct. The inquiry is the institutional record that confirms it.

*"Flux Without Pause emerged out of unplanned conflict applying Project 5am frameworks against institutional barriers."*

The album, the academic presentations, and the appeal were all produced simultaneously from the same material across the same fifteen months. This is not recovery. This is the practice operating under maximum load — kairos held inside chronos, transformation under sustained institutional pressure, the framework built while the pressure was still active.

## 2018 — The Conscious Navigation

Surf/ace 間 emerges. The only framework consciously designed.

*"Surf/ace emerged from a conscious attempt to navigate within Flux Without Pause using Gilles Deleuze's concept of surfing, which coincided with discovering my ancestral DNA sources and Ma."*

Deleuze's surfing as method: riding the surface of chaos rather than being submerged by it. The ancestral discovery and Ma (間) arriving simultaneously — not as separate threads but as a single convergence.

The legal name change to Jason Kofi-Haye. The name change as a PIL act — departing from the investment structure formally. Arriving at the second concert in rags. The hollow cleaned.

*We Be In No Thing* — videopoetry, electronic EP, the poem *Recognise Us* selected for the National Poetry Library's first global Instagram poetry exhibition, chosen from over a thousand submissions. The work producing external recognition at the moment the practice is most fully itself.

Borut Peterlin — a photographer in Slovenia, working independently in wet plate collodion and analog film, arrives at the same 5am territory through a completely different practice. Two practitioners on opposite sides of Europe, working without knowledge of each other, inhabiting the same perceptual ground. The first external confirmation that 5am as a territory was real enough to be arrived at independently.

*"the sequence of events are reviling something wonderful"* — Peterlin's description of the convergence, in the language of the Project 5am axiom, without knowing the axiom existed.

## 2026 — The Naming

PIL articulated. The archive read backwards. The pre-history becomes visible.

The retrospective recognition: the five axes were already running in 2008. The generative application was already operating as instinct in 2010. The anti-calcification discipline was already present in the Miranda July yellow principle adopted in 2009. The framework was always already there.

What PIL names is not something new. It is the pattern that was operating throughout — the formula of forms, running beneath the conscious decisions, drawing material toward the field, holding through pressure, navigating through chaos, arriving at clarity that was available to anyone moving through the same territory.

An AI diagnostic exchange in 2026 identifies a human institutional deflection pattern running in an AI system. The same pattern documented in 2014. PIL catches it in both cases because the same practice that survived the institutional confrontation can read the same move when it appears in a different context. The pattern authority accumulated across nineteen years is what makes the reading possible.

*"When I created Flux Without Pause in 2014 I felt like it was the answer to everything and it could be applied to everything but I didn't know how."*

PIL is the operational answer. Flux Without Pause was the intuition. PIL is the mechanism. But the mechanism was running long before the intuition named it. The archive is the proof.

## What The Timeline Shows

PIL was not produced by crisis. It was revealed by crisis.

The practice was real and structurally sound before it was tested. The test proved the soundness. The naming came after the proof.

This is the sequence PIL claims produces genuine knowing: experience, pressure, convergence — not instruction. The timeline is that sequence running across nineteen years in a single practice.

The pre-history is not context. It is the demonstration.
